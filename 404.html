About ICoN

About the Conference

The ICoN meetings are the premier opportunity to showcase research on nitrification and related processes in the nitrogen cycle. ICoN conferences bring together academic, government, and private-sector researchers to share scientific discoveries, innovations, and critical societal impacts of the global biogeochemical nitrogen cycle. The conference is held every two years and rotates locations between North America, Europe, and Asia.

In accordance with this tradition, ICoN10 will encompass a broad range of topics, including microbial physiology, metabolism, and biochemistry; biological nitrogen removal and greenhouse gas control; terrestrial nitrogen cycling; marine and freshwater nitrogen cycling; and advances in experimental and computational methods. We particularly encourage active participation by young investigators through an expanded Early Career Scientist program.
