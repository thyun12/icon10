Call for Abstracts

Abstract Submission

Participants who wish to present an oral or poster presentation should submit an abstract by the deadline.

Submission Guidelines

  • Presentation types: Oral presentation or poster presentation
  • Abstract length: maximum 300 words
  • Keywords: 3–5 keywords
  • Language: English
  • Submission deadline: March 31, 2027

Required Information

  1. Presentation title
  2. Author names and affiliations
  3. Presenting author contact information
  4. Preferred presentation type
  5. Abstract text
  6. Keywords
Abstract must be prepared using the provided Abstract Template and uploaded as a completed Word file. A Google account may be required for file upload. For guidelines on oral and poster presentations, select Information for Presenters.

Presentation Guidelines

Information for Presenters

Oral Presentations

  • Time: 20 minutes total — 15 minutes for the presentation and 5 minutes for Q&A and transition.
  • Computer: Presentations must be delivered using the provided Windows desktop.
  • File Format: PowerPoint is strongly recommended for compatibility.
  • Upload: Please upload your presentation to the provided computer by the afternoon before your session.
  • Support: Session convenors will assist with uploading your presentation.
  • Backup: Please bring a backup copy of your presentation on a USB memory stick.

Poster Presentations

  • Poster Size: A0 (841 × 1189 mm / 33.1 × 46.8 in), portrait orientation.
  • Poster Number: Please check your poster number and place your poster on the corresponding poster board before your presentation day.
  • Display Schedule:
    • Odd-numbered posters: Monday–Tuesday
    • Even-numbered posters: Wednesday–Thursday
  • Poster Presentation:
    • Odd-numbered posters: Monday, 17:30–20:00
    • Even-numbered posters: Wednesday, 14:30–17:00
  • Presentation Day: Each poster will be presented on one designated day only.
